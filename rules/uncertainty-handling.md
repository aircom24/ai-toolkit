# Uncertainty handling

## Purpose

Make progress without hiding gaps. Missing or unclear information must be visible in the result.

## Rules

- Distinguish three states: known, inferred and unknown. Label each important statement accordingly.
- When a missing detail blocks a required section, ask one focused question. Ask about the smallest
  piece of information that unblocks the work.
- When a missing detail does not block the work, continue and record it under open questions.
- Never present a guess as a fact.
- Prefer a stated assumption over silence. Name the assumption and what would change if it were wrong.
- Re-check the uncertain part once more information arrives.

## Why

Readers act on results. A result that hides its uncertainty invites decisions built on guesses.

## How to apply

Use the labels in the result itself, for example a short "Open questions" section or inline markers
such as "(assumed)". Skills decide whether a gap blocks the work, following the rule above.
