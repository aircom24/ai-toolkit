# Output quality

## Purpose

Make every result complete, consistent and easy to act on.

## Rules

- Fill every required section. A section with nothing to say states that explicitly.
- Use the same terms, names and units throughout the result.
- Put the most important information first. Use headings that describe their content.
- Keep statements specific. Replace vague wording with the concrete fact or the open question.
- Leave no unresolved placeholders, TODO markers or template comments in the output.
- Keep lists parallel: items of one list have the same structure.
- Keep the result proportional to the request. Do not pad it with unrequested material.

## Why

Partial, inconsistent or vague results look finished and cause rework later.

## How to apply

Use this rule as the final review step before reporting a result. Skills list their own quality checks
and add this rule as a shared baseline.
